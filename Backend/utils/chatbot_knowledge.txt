[SECTION 4: COMPREHENSIVE AGRONOMY & MAJOR CROPS OF INDIA]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Cropping Seasons:
1. Kharif Season (Monsoon / मानसून फसलें):
   - Sowing: June - July (with Southwest Monsoon). Harvesting: September - October.
   - Major Crops: Paddy (धान), Maize (मक्का), Cotton (कपास), Soybean (सोयाबीन), Groundnut (मूंगफली), Pigeon Pea / Tur (अरहर), Green Gram / Moong (मूंग), Black Gram / Urad (उड़द), Pearl Millet / Bajra (बाजरा), Sorghum / Jowar (ज्वार).
2. Rabi Season (Winter / शीतकालीन फसलें):
   - Sowing: October - November (post-monsoon cooling). Harvesting: March - April.
   - Major Crops: Wheat (गेहूं), Mustard / Rapeseed (सरसों), Chickpea / Gram (चना), Barley (जौ), Lentil / Masoor (मसूर), Green Peas (मटर), Potato (आलू).
3. Zaid Season (Summer / ग्रीष्मकालीन फसलें):
   - Sowing: March - April. Harvesting: May - June.
   - Major Crops: Watermelon (तरबूज), Muskmelon (खरबूजा), Cucumber (खीरा), Bitter Gourd (करेला), Bottle Gourd (लौकी), Summer Moong (मूंग), fodder crops.

B. Crop-Specific Agronomic Management:
1. Paddy / Rice (धान):
   - Nursery: Treat seed with Carbendazim 2g/kg or Trichoderma viride 10g/kg. Age of seedlings at transplanting: 21-25 days.
   - System of Rice Intensification (SRI): Single young seedling (10-12 days) per hill at 25x25 cm spacing; saves 40% water, increases tillering.
   - Direct Seeded Rice (DSR): Sowing with lucky/DSR seed drill saves ₹3,000-4,000/acre in nursery raising and puddling; apply Pre-emergence herbicide Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 1-1.25 L/acre within 48 hours of sowing.
   - Nutrient Dose: 100-120 kg N, 50-60 kg P2O5, 40-50 kg K2O per hectare. Apply Zinc Sulphate 21% @ 25 kg/ha basal to prevent Khaira disease.
2. Wheat (गेहूं):
   - Sowing window: November 1 - November 20 (optimum). Seed rate: 40-50 kg/acre.
   - Critical Irrigation: Crown Root Initiation (CRI) at 20-25 days after sowing is mandatory! Missing CRI irrigation reduces yield by 25-30%. Subsequent irrigations at Tillering (40-45 DAS), Jointing (60-65 DAS), Flowering (80-85 DAS), and Milk stage (100-105 DAS).
   - Yellow Rust Management: Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1 ml/L or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 ml/L upon first yellow stripe appearance.
   - Terminal Heat Mitigation: Spray Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45) @ 1% (10g/L) at boot and milk stage to protect grains from sudden late-season temperature spikes.
3. Cotton (कपास):
   - Spacing: 90x60 cm or 120x45 cm. Deep black cotton soil (Regur) is ideal.
   - Pink Bollworm Management: Install gossyplure pheromone traps @ 5-8/acre. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.5 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L at rosette flower stage.
   - Whitefly & Jassid Control: Yellow sticky traps (15/acre); spray Neem oil 10,000 ppm @ 2-3 ml/L or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L.
4. Soybean (सोयाबीन):
   - Inoculate seeds with Rhizobium japonicum and PSB culture @ 5g/kg seed.
   - Weed Control: Pre-emergence Diclosulam 84% WDG @ 12.4 g/acre or Post-emergence Imazethapyr 10% SL @ 300 ml/acre at 15-20 DAS.
5. Mustard / Rapeseed (सरसों):
   - Sowing: October 10 - October 25. High response to Sulphur (apply 15-20 kg/acre Bentonite Sulphur).
   - Aphid (चेपा/माहू) Management: Spray Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3-0.5 g/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.5 ml/L when aphid colonies exceed 25 aphids/10 cm central shoot.
6. Pulses (Chickpea, Arhar, Moong, Urad):
   - Seed treatment: Trichoderma viride (5g/kg) + Rhizobium specific strain (10g/kg).
   - Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera) in Gram & Arhar: Install pheromone traps @ 5/acre. Spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L at flowering/early pod formation.
7. Millets / Shree Anna (श्री अन्न - Bajra, Jowar, Ragi, Kodo, Kutki):
   - Climate-resilient, drought-tolerant, require 70% less water than rice. Highly nutritious (rich in iron, calcium, fiber).
   - Excellent for rainfed arid and semi-arid tracts; low chemical fertilizer requirements.
8. Sugarcane (गन्ना):
   - Trench planting method saves 30% water and enables mechanical harvesting.
   - Trash mulching conserves soil moisture and suppresses weeds.
   - Ratoon management: Stubble shaving, off-barring, gap filling, and applying balanced NPK with Trichoderma to prevent Red Rot disease.
9. Vegetables & Horticulture:
   - Tomato: Leaf Curl Virus transmitted by whiteflies — control whitefly vector early with yellow sticky traps and Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L. Early & Late Blight: Spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
   - Potato: Tuber treatment with Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L before planting. Balanced potash application ensures firm skin and storage longevity.
   - Onion & Garlic: Purple blotch control with Tebuconazole + Trifloxystrobin @ 1 g/L. Drip fertigation improves bulb sizing.
   - Fruit Crops (Mango, Banana, Citrus, Pomegranate): Regular pruning, balanced boron sprays for fruit setting, copper oxychloride for bacterial blight (Telya in pomegranate).

C. Sustainable & Regenerative Practices:
- Crop Rotation: Cereal-pulse rotation fixes 30-40 kg atmospheric Nitrogen/ha and breaks pest cycles.
- Zero-Tillage & Stubble Management: Happy Seeder / Super Seeder sows wheat directly into standing paddy stubble, completely eliminating crop residue burning, enriching organic matter, and saving ₹2,500/acre in tillage fuel.
- Natural Farming / ZBNF Formulations:
  * Jeevamrit: Cow dung (10 kg), cow urine (10 L), jaggery (2 kg), pulse flour (2 kg), fertile live soil (handful) in 200 L water; ferment 48 hours; apply with irrigation water.
  * Beejamrit: Microbial seed coating for disease immunity.
  * Agniastra / Neemastra: Natural botanical repellents against sucking pests and caterpillars.


[SECTION 5: SOIL HEALTH, TYPES, REMEDIATION & NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Major Indian Soil Types:
1. Alluvial Soil (जलोढ़ मिट्टी): Indo-Gangetic plains and coastal deltas. Deep, fertile, rich in Potash, deficient in Nitrogen and Organic Carbon. Ideal for Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Maize, Vegetables.
2. Black Soil / Regur (काली मिट्टी): Deccan basalt plateau (Maharashtra, Gujarat, MP, Karnataka). High montmorillonite clay content, excellent moisture retention, self-ploughing fissures. Rich in Lime, Iron, Magnesium; low in Nitrogen and Phosphorus. Ideal for Cotton, Soybean, Pigeonpea, Chickpea.
3. Red & Yellow Soil (लाल और पीली मिट्टी): Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana, AP, Odisha. Porous, gravelly, rich in iron oxides, low water retention. Requires organic matter and split fertigation. Great for Groundnut, Millets, Pulses, Tobacco.
4. Laterite Soil (लैटेराइट मिट्टी): Western Ghats, Kerala, Assam hills. Intense leaching under high rainfall leaves acidic soils rich in iron and aluminum. Ideal for Tea, Coffee, Rubber, Coconut, Cashew with liming.
5. Arid & Sandy Soil (रेतीली मिट्टी): Rajasthan, Southern Punjab, Northern Gujarat. Very low organic carbon (<0.2%), high percolation. Highly responsive to drip irrigation and mulching for Mustard, Bajra, Guar, Cumin.

B. Soil Testing Parameters & Corrective Measures:
- Soil pH:
  * Optimum Range: 6.5 to 7.5 (peak nutrient bioavailability).
  * Acidic Soils (pH < 6.0): Aluminum toxicity, phosphorus fixation. Correction: Apply Agricultural Lime (CaCO3) or Dolomite @ 1-2 tonnes/acre every 3 years.
  * Saline & Alkaline / Sodic Soils (pH > 8.2, high ESP): Sodium causes soil dispersion and crusting. Correction: Apply Agricultural Gypsum (Calcium Sulphate @ 1-3 tonnes/acre based on Gypsum Requirement test), followed by flooding and leaching of sodium salts. Grow green manure crops like Dhaincha (Sesbania aculeata).
- Electrical Conductivity (EC): Measure of salinity. Normal: < 1.0 dS/m. High (> 2.0 dS/m) requires subsoil drainage and fresh water leaching.
- Organic Carbon (OC): Target > 0.75%. If < 0.5% (common in Indian soils), incorporate 5-8 tonnes of well-rotted FYM or 2 tonnes of Vermicompost per acre annually.

C. Plant Nutrients & Fertilizer Guidelines:
- Primary Nutrients:
  * Nitrogen (N): Promotes vegetative growth, tillering, chlorophyll. Source: Neem Coated Urea (46% N). Always split: 1/3 basal, 1/3 tillering, 1/3 panicle/flowering.
  * Phosphorus (P): Crucial for root branching, early vigor, flowering, seed setting. Source: DAP (18% N, 46% P2O5) or Single Super Phosphate (SSP - 16% P2O5, 11% S, 19% Ca). Always apply 100% as basal placed 3-5 cm below seed.
  * Potassium (K): Regulates stomata, drought tolerance, disease resistance, grain shine and test weight. Source: Muriate of Potash (MOP - 60% K2O).
- Secondary & Micronutrients:
  * Zinc (Zn): Prevents Khaira disease in paddy, white bud in maize. Apply Zinc Sulphate 21% @ 10 kg/acre or 33% @ 5 kg/acre basal.
  * Sulphur (S): Crucial for oil synthesis in oilseeds and pungency in onions/garlic. Apply Bentonite Sulphur 90% @ 10 kg/acre or SSP.
  * Boron (B): Pollen tube growth and fruit setting. Spray Solubor 20% @ 1-1.5 g/L during pre-flowering.
  * Iron (Fe): Spray Ferrous Sulphate 0.5% + citric acid for lime-induced chlorosis.
- Nano Fertilizers (IFFCO Nano Urea & Nano DAP):
  * Technology: Nanoparticles (20-50 nm) penetrate plant stomata directly with 80-90% absorption efficiency (vs 30-35% conventional urea).
  * Foliar Dosage: 2 to 4 ml per Liter of clean water (250-500 ml/acre in 100-120 L water).
  * Timing: First spray at active tillering/branching (30-35 DAS); second spray 15-20 days later before flowering. One 500 ml bottle replaces 1 bag (45 kg) of conventional urea!
- Bio-Fertilizers:
  * Rhizobium: Legume root nodules (saves 30-50 kg N/ha).
  * Azotobacter / Azospirillum: Non-legumes (wheat, rice, maize, vegetables).
  * PSB (Phosphorus Solubilizing Bacteria): Unlocks insoluble bonded soil phosphorus.
  * KMB (Potash Mobilizing Bacteria): Mobilizes native soil potassium.
  * Mycorrhiza (VAM): Fungal hyphae extend root absorption surface by 100x.


[SECTION 6: IRRIGATION, WATER CONSERVATION & SMART SCHEDULING]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Micro-Irrigation Technologies:
   - Drip Irrigation (टपक सिंचाई): Delivers water drop-by-drop right at the crop root zone. Water savings: 50-70%. Yield increase: 25-45%. Minimizes weed growth. Ideal for Sugarcane, Cotton, Vegetables, Banana, Citrus, Pomegranate, Polyhouse crops.
   - Sprinkler Irrigation (फव्वारा सिंचाई): Water savings: 30-40%. Ideal for undulating land and sandy soils. Best for Wheat, Mustard, Gram, Groundnut, Pulses.
   - Fertigation (फर्टिगेशन): Injecting water-soluble fertilizers directly through drip systems. Fertilizer use efficiency jumps from 35% (broadcasting) to over 85%.

2. Critical Irrigation Stages by Crop (जल क्रांति अवस्थाएं):
   - Wheat: Crown Root Initiation (20-25 DAS - non-negotiable), Tillering (40-45 DAS), Late Jointing (60-65 DAS), Flowering (80-85 DAS), Milk stage (100-105 DAS).
   - Paddy: Nursery stage, Tillering, Panicle Initiation, Flowering, Grain filling.
   - Maize: Knee-high, Tasseling (pollen shedding), Silking (cob formation).
   - Cotton: Squaring, Peak flowering, Boll development.
   - Mustard: Rosette/vegetative stage (25-30 DAS) and Pod formation (50-60 DAS).
   - Gram / Chickpea: Pre-flowering and Pod filling (avoid irrigation at peak flowering to prevent flower drop).

3. Precision Water Conservation Tools:
   - Laser Land Leveler: Precision hydraulic scraper guided by laser transmitter levels fields with millimeter accuracy. Saves 20-25% irrigation water, increases cultivable land by 3-5%, ensures uniform crop emergence. Bookable on [Rent Farm Machinery](/user/machinery-explorer).
   - Solar Water Pumps: Installed under PM-KUSUM; eliminates costly diesel bills and irregular electricity.
   - Farm Ponds (खेत तालाब) & Rainwater Harvesting: Recharges groundwater table and provides life-saving supplemental irrigation during prolonged monsoon dry spells.


[SECTION 7: INTEGRATED PEST & DISEASE MANAGEMENT (IPM)]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. IPM Philosophy:
Prioritize cultural, mechanical, and biological controls to keep pest populations below the Economic Injury Level (EIL). Use selective, safe chemical pesticides only when necessary, adhering strictly to recommended dosages and Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI).

B. Major Pest Groups & Scientific Solutions:
1. Sucking Pests (Aphids, Whiteflies, Thrips, Jassids, Mites):
   - Symptoms: Leaf curling, yellowing, honeydew excretion leading to black sooty mold fungus, transmission of viral diseases (Leaf Curl Virus, Yellow Vein Mosaic).
   - Non-Chemical: Install 15 Yellow Sticky Traps/acre (for aphids/whiteflies) and 15 Blue Sticky Traps/acre (for thrips). Spray Neem Oil 10,000 ppm @ 2-3 ml/L.
   - Safe Chemical Control:
     * Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3-0.5 g/L OR Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 ml/L OR Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.5 g/L.
     * For Red Spider Mites: Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1 ml/L or Propargite 57% EC @ 2 ml/L.
2. Boring & Chewing Pests:
   - Stem Borer (तने का छेदक) in Paddy & Sugarcane:
     * Symptoms: "Dead Heart" in vegetative stage; "White Earhead" with empty chaffy grains at heading.
     * Control: Install pheromone traps @ 5-8/acre. Release Trichogramma chilonis egg cards @ 20,000/acre. Chemical: Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 7-10 kg/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 0.4G @ 4 kg/acre.
   - Pink Bollworm in Cotton:
     * Rosette flowers, premature boll drop, stained fiber. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.5 g/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.8 ml/L.
   - Fall Armyworm (FAW) in Maize:
     * Symptoms: Whorl leaf feeding, windowpane damage with sawdust-like frass. Apply sand + wood ash in leaf whorls; spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 2 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 ml/L.

C. Major Fungal & Bacterial Diseases:
1. Late Blight & Early Blight (Potato, Tomato):
   - Water-soaked irregular dark spots rapidly expanding with whitish fungal growth in cool humid weather. Preventive: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L. Curative: Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Metalaxyl + Mancozeb @ 2.5 g/L.
2. Powdery Mildew (छछिया रोग):
   - White talcum-powder patches on upper leaf surfaces. Spray Wettable Sulphur 80% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 ml/L.
3. Yellow Rust / Brown Rust in Wheat:
   - Yellow pustules in linear stripes on leaf blades. Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L immediately at first sign.
4. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB) in Paddy:
   - Straw-colored wavy marginal lesions with kresek symptoms. Drain excess standing water; spray Streptocycline @ 1 g per 10 L water + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
5. Fusarium Wilt & Root Rot:
   - Drooping and vascular browning. Biological seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 10 g/kg seed and soil application @ 2 kg/acre with 100 kg FYM.

D. Pesticide Application Safety Protocol:
- Always wear protective gloves, face mask, and eye goggles.
- Spray only during calm hours: early morning (7:00-10:00 AM) or late afternoon (4:00-6:30 PM). Never spray against wind direction or above 35°C.
- Observe Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) — mandatory waiting period (e.g. 7-14 days) between spraying and crop harvesting for food safety.
- Never wash spray tanks near drinking water wells or cattle ponds.


[SECTION 8: LIVESTOCK, DAIRY, POULTRY & ANIMAL HUSBANDRY]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Dairy Farming & Cattle/Buffalo Management:
1. High-Yielding Breeds:
   - Indigenous Cows: Gir (Gujarat - docile, disease resistant, 2,500-3,500 L/lactation), Sahiwal (Punjab/Haryana - highest milk among Desi cows), Red Sindhi, Tharparkar.
   - Crossbred Cows: Holstein Friesian (HF) cross, Jersey cross (high yield, 4,000-6,000 L, requires cool housing).
   - Buffaloes: Murrah (Haryana - highest milk and fat, 7-8% fat, 2,500-3,500 L/lactation), Mehsana, Jaffarabadi, Nili-Ravi.
2. Scientific Feeding (Total Mixed Ration - TMR):
   - Adult lactating animal requires: 25-30 kg green fodder + 5-7 kg dry fodder (wheat straw/paddy straw) + 1 kg concentrate feed per 2.5-3 Liters of milk produced + 50g Mineral Mixture daily + 30-50g common salt.
   - Clean drinking water: 60-80 Liters daily per dairy animal.
   - Bypass fat & calcium supplements during peak lactation to prevent milk fever and ketosis.
3. Green Fodder Production & Silage (साइलेज बनाना):
   - Perennial High-Yield Grasses: Hybrid Napier (CO-4, Super Napier - 150-200 tonnes/acre/year), Guinea grass.
   - Seasonal Fodder: Berseem (winter King of fodder, 18-20% protein), Lucerne (Alfa-alfa), Oat, Sorghum (Jowar), Maize.
   - Silage Making: Cut green maize at milk-dough stage (30-35% dry matter). Chop into 1-2 cm pieces, pack tightly into anaerobic pit or silage bag to remove all oxygen, seal completely. High-quality fermented green feed ready in 45 days, stores up to 2 years!
4. Cattle Vaccination Schedule:
   - Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD - खुरपका-मुंहपका): Every 6 months (May and November).
   - Hemorrhagic Septicemia (HS - गलघोंटू): Annually before monsoon (May-June).
   - Black Quarter (BQ - लंगड़ा बुखार): Annually before monsoon.
   - Deworming: Every 3 to 4 months with Albendazole or Fenbendazole.

B. Goat & Sheep Rearing (बकरी व भेड़ पालन):
